# Resources and acknowledgments

This collection is a practical starting point, not an exhaustive literature review or an endorsement of every detail in each linked source. Verify that linked resources remain current. Contributions of additional evidence-based resources are welcome.

## Community allyship and inclusive communication

- [Jorge Ordovás — DEI Companion](https://github.com/joobid/DEI-companion) — practical companion for ongoing allyship, text review, learning, and actions. Thanks to Jorge for the inspiration and shared references.
- [Jorge's curated DEI references](https://github.com/joobid/DEI-companion/blob/main/references/BIBLIOTECA_DEI.md) — further resources and evidence to explore; refer to the original repository for attribution and updates.
- [Better Allies](https://betterallies.com/) — advice and resources for everyday allyship.
- [What Works — Iris Bohnet](https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674986565) — research-oriented book about designing organizations to address bias.

## Inclusive events and speaking

- [CHAOSS DEI Event Badging, version 4.0](https://www.chaoss.community/diversity-and-inclusion-badging/) — publicly documented categories and human peer-review program for open-source events. Our event review is **independent**, with no scoring or official badging.
- [OpenNews Speaker Rider](https://opennews.org/projects/speaker-rider/) — concrete expectations speakers can bring to event conversations.
- [Creating my inclusive speaking rider — Imma Valls](https://eyeveebee.dev/creating-my-inclusive-speaking-rider) — a speaker-led approach to stating inclusion expectations.
- [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://www.cncf.io/community/code-of-conduct/) — useful example; **do not presume** a particular event has adopted it without verification.
