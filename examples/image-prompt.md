# Example: community meetup image prompt

**Prompt:**

> Create a realistic editorial illustration of attendees chatting at a cloud-native meetup in Barcelona around pizza and drinks after technical talks. Show a natural mix of adults with varied visible ages, skin tones, and gender presentations, with several people actively discussing technology rather than posing. Avoid stereotypes about who is technical or leading the conversation. Give individuals distinct expressions and roles. Make the venue plausible and welcoming; preserve a realistic documentary feel. No invented sponsorship logos or textual claims.

**Review after generation:** Is everyone shown as a similar young man? Are people of different appearances all actively participating? Has the model assigned organizing or service roles disproportionately? Revise the prompt and image as needed, rather than accepting the first output.

*Illustrative text only; no generated image is included as proof.*
