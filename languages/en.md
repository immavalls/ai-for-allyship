# English: inclusive writing notes

- Use natural gender-neutral occupational terms ('chair', 'speaker', 'developer', 'organizer') when gender is irrelevant.
- Prefer 'everyone', 'folks', 'attendees', 'participants', or 'team' where appropriate over generic 'guys'.
- Do not assume pronouns, relationships, disability, or professional rank. Ask for name and pronouns if personally relevant; otherwise rewrite to avoid assumptions.
- Prefer invitations that describe the activity and what attendees can expect rather than whether people are elite enough to attend.
- Keep plain-language explanations and avoid overcorrecting into artificial or patronizing wording.
- Different communities have different conventions; ask the organizer when context matters.
