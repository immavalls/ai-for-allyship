# Guidance: community safeguards in event content

When drafting or reviewing event material, check whether the information below is available. These are prompts for organizers, not proof of an event's safety.

- **Code of Conduct:** direct, working link to an existing policy applicable to this event; don't invent one or silently paste a generic policy as if adopted.
- **Reporting and response:** contact/reporting route and enough information to understand what happens next. Avoid naming a reporting contact without confirmation.
- **Accessibility:** venue access, captions/interpretation where relevant, seating/quiet space, accommodation request mechanism, and contact details as provided.
- **Cost and participation:** clear registration, pricing, scholarships or supported tickets where offered, and eligibility/access constraints.
- **Practical inclusion:** schedule, location/remote access, dietary needs or family arrangements when relevant and confirmed.
- **Tone:** avoid unnecessary exclusivity or prestige language, including in image captions and ticket descriptions.

**Drafting rule:** If missing, append a short checklist for the organizer rather than publishing invented facts. **Review rule:** Distinguish 'not located on inspected pages' from 'does not exist'.

Link to a Code of Conduct is a minimum discoverability check, not proof the policy is enforced or that the event is safe.
